## Where to Add Hashtags on YouTube Videos?

You can add YouTube hashtags in these main places:

1. **Video Title:** Add relevant hashtags to your video title to highlight its main topic. Keep the title short and avoid using too many hashtags.

2. **Video Description:** Add hashtags at the beginning or end of your video description. YouTube may display up to three hashtags from the description above the video title.

3. **YouTube Shorts Description:** Include relevant hashtags in your Shorts description to describe your short-form content and its topic.

4. **During Video Upload:** Open YouTube Studio, enter your video details, and add hashtags in the title or description field before publishing.

**Tip:** Use relevant hashtags only. YouTube ignores all hashtags on a video if it has more than 60.

## How Many Hashtags Should You Use on YouTube?

Using 3–5 relevant hashtags per YouTube video is a simple approach. Choose hashtags that describe your video topic, main keyword, and target audience. You can combine popular hashtags with niche-specific ones to make your content easier to understand.

Avoid adding too many or unrelated hashtags just to attract views. YouTube ignores all hashtags if a video has more than 60. Focus on quality and relevance rather than quantity.

## Common YouTube Hashtag Mistakes to Avoid

- **Using Irrelevant Hashtags:** Avoid hashtags that do not match your video topic.
- **Adding Too Many Hashtags:** Don't overload your video with hashtags. YouTube ignores all hashtags if you use more than 60.
- **Ignoring Niche-Specific Hashtags:** Use hashtags related to your content category.
- **Copying Competitor Hashtags:** Choose hashtags that match your own video instead of copying others.
- **Using Misspelled Hashtags:** Check spelling before publishing your video.
- **Repeating the Same Hashtags:** Select hashtags according to each video's topic.
- **Following Every Trend:** Use trending hashtags only when they are relevant to your content.
